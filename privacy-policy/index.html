Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DigiNexus ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our mobile applications and games published on Google Play under the developer name DigiNexus (the "Apps"), and this website, digi-nexus.org (the "Website"). By using our Apps or Website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we and our partners may process

Depending on the App and the features you use, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Account information. If an App offers sign-in (for example through Firebase Authentication), we may process your email address, display name, and a unique user ID, as provided by the sign-in provider.
  • Device and technical information. Device model, operating system version, language, region, app version, and other technical identifiers.
  • Advertising identifiers. The Android Advertising ID and similar identifiers used to show and measure ads.
  • Usage and analytics data. How you interact with an App, screens viewed, features used, session length, and in-app events.
  • Diagnostics and crash data. Crash reports, error logs and performance data used to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Content you create in an App. For example, characters, designs, saves and settings you create. This content is generally stored on your device; if an App offers cloud features, it may be stored with our service providers so it can sync for you.
  • Support communications. If you email us, we process your email address and the contents of your message so we can reply.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, and our Apps do not require you to provide information beyond what is needed for the features you choose to use.

2. Third-party services and advertising

Our Apps use third-party software development kits (SDKs) and services for advertising, authentication, analytics, crash reporting and app functionality. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and may collect device information, advertising identifiers, usage data and diagnostics. Depending on the App, these providers include:

  • Google AdMob and Google Play services (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase (sign-in, crash reporting, analytics) — Firebase Privacy information
  • Yandex advertising and analytics services — Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Facebook) advertising and analytics SDKs — Meta Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads and Unity services — Unity Privacy Policy
  • Other advertising mediation and analytics partners that may be integrated in a specific App from time to time.

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your device settings, your region, and the consent choices presented in the App where applicable. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID and opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or the equivalent on your device).

3. How information is used

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Apps and their features;
  • To show advertising and measure its performance, which keeps our Apps free to use;
  • To understand how the Apps are used and improve them;
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes, bugs and abuse;
  • To respond to your support requests;
  • To comply with legal obligations and Google Play policies.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above for the purposes in this policy, when required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or property.

5. Data retention and deletion

Analytics, advertising and diagnostic data is retained by our providers for the periods set in their own policies. Account and support data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. You may request deletion of data associated with you at any time — see our Data Deletion page or email us at kaleemullahabc700@gmail.com.

7. Children

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact us at kaleemullahabc700@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

This Website is a static information site. It does not run user accounts, does not set advertising cookies of its own, and does not collect personal information unless you choose to email us.
