Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with your use of DigiNexus apps at any time, without deleting your Google Play account.

How to request deletion

  • Send an email to kaleemullahabc700@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  • Tell us the name of the app, and — if you signed in to the app — the email address or user ID of the account you used, so we can find the data.
  • We will confirm receipt, delete the associated account and personal data held by us, and confirm completion. Requests are normally completed within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what may be kept

  • Deleted: account information (such as email and user ID) and personal data we hold that is associated with you.
  • Kept where required: anonymised analytics, crash and advertising data held by our service providers (such as Google, Firebase, Yandex, Meta and Unity) is retained and expires under their own policies; you can reset your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device settings. Certain records may be kept where the law requires it.
  • On your device: uninstalling an app removes its locally stored data from your device.

See our Privacy Policy for full details of how data is handled.
